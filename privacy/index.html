Veykra Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Veykra collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Veykra apps for macOS, iPhone, and iPad, related cloud services, subscription features, and support channels.

Veykra helps creators turn longer videos into short social clips with captions, editing controls, and AI-assisted generation. The app includes both cloud-backed production features and local/mock modes used for demos, testing, and review.

Information We Collect

We collect information needed to provide the app and operate the service:

  • Account information: Apple sign-in tokens, authorization codes, and Apple-provided profile information such as email address, given name, and family name when Apple makes those fields available.
  • Session information: access tokens, expiration times, and authenticated user records needed to keep you signed in.
  • Project and video metadata: source title, source kind, language, duration, file size, target platform, preferred clip length, and brand preset choices.
  • Source video files: local videos you choose to upload when using cloud-backed generation.
  • Generated content: generation job status, generated clip metadata, captions, titles, timing, edits, export settings, and related workspace content.
  • Subscription and credit information: product identifiers, entitlement state, free credits, monthly subscription allowance, purchased credits, transaction-related records, and billing status needed to unlock paid features.
  • Technical information: app version, device platform, request metadata, logs, and diagnostics needed for security, reliability, fraud prevention, and support.
  • Support information: information you provide when you contact us for help, privacy requests, or account support.

Mock mode and local preview flows use deterministic local data and do not intentionally upload videos or consume credits. Cloud mode may upload the selected source video and project metadata to Veykra's backend so the requested generation job can run.

How We Use Information

We use information to provide, maintain, and improve Veykra; authenticate users; upload selected source videos for cloud-backed generation; create, process, poll, and deliver generation jobs; maintain projects, captions, edits, and export settings; manage subscriptions and credits; provide support; prevent abuse; and comply with legal, accounting, tax, platform, and App Store obligations.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use source videos, captions, generated clips, or project content for third-party advertising.

Video and AI Processing

When you use cloud-backed generation, Veykra may upload and process the video file you selected, associated metadata, captions, and generation settings. Processing may include transcription, caption generation, content analysis, clip selection, reframing, rendering preparation, and other AI-assisted video workflow steps.

Cloud processing may be performed by Veykra-operated systems and service providers that help host, store, analyze, transcribe, generate, render, monitor, and deliver the service. Service providers are permitted to process information only as needed to provide services to Veykra and must protect it according to their agreements with us.

When you use local/mock mode, Veykra uses local sample data or local device state for the preview experience. Local/mock mode is intended for demos, testing, and review and does not intentionally upload source videos or charge credits.

Subscriptions and Payments

Veykra may offer auto-renewing subscriptions, monthly credits, promotional free credits, or other paid features. Purchases on Apple platforms are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple may provide Veykra with transaction and entitlement information needed to unlock features, prevent fraud, handle refunds, provide support, and maintain accurate credit balances.

Payment card numbers and App Store account payment details are handled by Apple and are not collected by Veykra.

Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain your account, support subscriptions and credits, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and enforce agreements.

You may request account deletion from inside the app. When account deletion is processed, Veykra deletes or deactivates account records and associated service data unless retention is required for security, legal, tax, accounting, fraud prevention, payment, backup, or platform compliance reasons. Some records may remain in backups for a limited period before they are overwritten through normal backup rotation.

Deleting the Veykra account does not automatically cancel an Apple subscription. You can manage or cancel Apple subscriptions through your Apple Account subscription settings.

Your Choices

You can choose whether to sign in with Apple, upload a source video, use cloud generation, or use local/mock flows where available. You can also manage App Store subscriptions through Apple, delete your account in the app, and contact us about privacy requests.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, export, restrict, or object to certain processing of your personal information. We will respond to privacy requests as required by applicable law.

Security

We use technical, administrative, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and loss. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

International Processing

Veykra and its service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for cross-border transfers.

Children's Privacy

Veykra is not intended for children under 13 or for users below the minimum age required by local law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to Veykra, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the date above and provide additional notice when required by law.

Contact Us

For privacy questions or requests, contact Veykra at privacy@veykra.ai.
