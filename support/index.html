Veykra Support

Need help with Veykra? Contact support at support@veykra.ai.

Account and billing

For questions about Sign in with Apple, subscriptions, monthly credits, promotional credits, purchase state, refunds, or account access, email support@veykra.ai.

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple Account subscription settings. Deleting your Veykra account does not automatically cancel an Apple subscription.

Generation and uploads

For help with video import, cloud uploads, generation jobs, generated clips, captions, editor behavior, or export preparation, include the device platform, app version, source video length, and a short description of what happened.

Mock mode is available for testing and review. It does not intentionally upload source videos or consume credits.

Privacy and deletion

For privacy questions, account deletion, or data requests, contact privacy@veykra.ai. You can also request account deletion from inside the app.

Feedback and feature requests

For product feedback, creator workflow requests, and feature ideas, email support@veykra.ai.
